Marwa Gouda is an Arabic–English conference interpreter based in Cairo. She interprets simultaneously and consecutively at conferences, high-level meetings and consultations, with a focus on politics, international affairs, human security and refugee issues. She holds the Professional Diploma in Simultaneous and Consecutive Interpreting from the American University in Cairo.

Before the booth, she spent thirteen years translating and editing for international brands from English and German into Arabic, and she works as an Arabic voice-over artist. Her voice is trained for broadcast, and she is open to television, live studio and sports assignments.

Interpreting & Translation

Conference interpreting · Arabic ⇄ English

Simultaneous and consecutive, on site or remote. Each event gets its own glossary, prepared from the agenda and speakers' papers. Documents stay confidential, and NDAs are welcome.

Translation & voice-over · English, German → Arabic

Translation, editing, post-editing and subtitling for agencies and brands, in marketing, automotive, technical, IT and e-learning content. And an Arabic voice in Modern Standard or Egyptian for commercials, narration, e-learning and IVR, with a team of male and female voice talents, copywriters and video editors for bigger projects.

Interpreters at the Notary Public (Shahr El-Aqari)

Foreign clients completing a transaction at a Notary Public office in Cairo can book a certified interpreter to attend with them, at find-an-interpreter.com.
